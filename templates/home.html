Collaboration

This project began in 2024 as an experiment in neural style transfer and classical image processing.

Suggestions and reproducible bug reports are welcome through the project repository.

Limitations

Neural models operate at fixed input resolutions. Images are resized for processing, so outputs may lose detail or show artifacts.

Processing time depends on the host machine. JPEG and PNG uploads are limited to 10 MiB and 12 megapixels per image.

Images are processed on the server. Optional image archiving and activity tracking require your consent.
