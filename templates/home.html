Limitations

You might notice some funky images being generated here :) Well, that's because Generative AI is not yet matured and still an evolving field in Computer Science.

Additionally, there's some compression applied to the image processing system. You can imagine a video that should be 80 MB squished down to just 4 MB, it will obviously impact the resulting video quality.

Now, you might wonder why we need to deal with all this compression stuff. Well, to render high-quality pics, we need a ton of computer resources, and the server that runs StyleDreamer has limited resources :(

So, StyleDreamer has to be savvy with its resources. But hey, it's pushing boundaries and getting there, one pixel at a time!
